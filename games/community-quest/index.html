How to Play

  • Move: A / D or Left / Right arrows (touch: Left and Right buttons).
  • Jump: Space, W, or Up arrow (touch: Jump).
  • Explore a station: stand near it and press E or Enter, or tap its prompt (touch: Explore).
  • Pause: Esc, or the Pause button.

Goal: light each station in a level to collect its chapter token and open the gate. Insight Sparks are optional.

Falling returns you to the last checkpoint. Nothing is lost.
